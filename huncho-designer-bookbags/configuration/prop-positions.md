# Prop positions (/bagfix)

Default positions are already tuned. Use `/bagfix` only if a bag sits wrong on your server's clothing or peds.

1. Type `/bagfix` (or `/bagfix mini`). A preview bag appears on your character.
2. Pick **Bookbag** or **Mini**, a style (**Back**, **Chest**, **One strap**) and a part (**Bag**, **Left strap**, **Right strap**).
3. Use **+** and **−** to move and rotate it. **Fine**, **Normal** and **Big** change the step size, and **Turn me** rotates your character.
4. Press **Save fix**. Every change also saves when you switch part, style or size, or close the panel.
5. Repeat on a female character. Male and female positions save separately.

**Copy fixes** copies every saved position as text. It is also written to `data/prop_fixes.json` and printed in the server console.

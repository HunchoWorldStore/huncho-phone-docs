# Requirements

* **Framework:** Qbox, QBCore or ESX, or your own through the [custom bridge](../integrations/custom-scripts.md)
* **Inventory:** ox_inventory, qb-inventory, qs-inventory, wasabi_inventory, tgiann-inventory, ak47_inventory, ps-inventory or lj-inventory, or your own
* **Target (optional):** ox_target or qb-target for shop clerks. Without one, players press **E** at the shop
* **Notifications (optional):** ox_lib is used when it is running

No database tables are needed. Shops and positions save in the server's key-value storage.

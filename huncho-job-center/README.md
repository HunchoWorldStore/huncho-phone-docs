# Huncho Job Center

**Huncho Job Center** (`hunchoworld_jobcenter`) gives your city 30 civilian jobs that look and feel like real work. Players sign up at the Job Center, report to the depot, change into a uniform, take a work vehicle and work through tasks with real props and animations. When they clock out they get a pay stub and a paycheck.

`Qbox` · `QBCore` · `ESX Legacy` · `ox_lib + oxmysql` · `50+ scripts detected`

## Highlights

* **30 jobs in 4 categories:** labor, driving, service, and outdoor & trades. Seven of them have their own full mechanics (fishing, prospecting, dog walking, paperboy, mover, order picker, tow truck).
* **Crews:** work a shift together, sized to the seats in the job's vehicle, with a crew pay bonus.
* **Job of the day, work streaks and leaderboards** to bring players back.
* **Optional level system:** XP per task, jobs unlocked by level, and a pay bonus per job level.
* **In-game editors:** move every location with `/jclocations` and set every uniform with `/jcuniforms`. Saved in the database and kept after restarts.
* **Staff tools:** live shift list, a pay overview with real $/hour per job, and Discord logs with flags for failed server checks.
* **Server-checked pay:** the server picks every route and checks every task.
* **Works with what you run:** Qbox, QBCore or ESX, plus 50+ target, inventory, banking, key, fuel and notification scripts, detected automatically. Anything else can be hooked up in one file.

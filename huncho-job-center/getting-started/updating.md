# Updating

1. Download the new version from [Keymaster](https://keymaster.fivem.net). Bought it on the [Huncho World FiveM Store](https://huncho-world-fivem-store.tebex.store/)? It shows up there under your Cfx account.
2. **Keep your config.** Back up `config/` (and `bridge/custom.lua` if you filled it in), replace the resource folder, then put your files back. Check the [changelog](../changelog.md) for new settings to copy over.
3. Restart the resource. New database tables and columns are added automatically.

Locations moved with `/jclocations` and uniforms set with `/jcuniforms` live in the database, so updating never wipes them.
